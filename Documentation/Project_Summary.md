# Project Summary

## Project Name

Healthcare Sales & Profitability Analysis Dashboard

## Objective

The objective of this project was to develop an interactive Power BI dashboard to analyze healthcare business performance across revenue, cost, profitability and product returns.

## Business Questions

1. How is revenue performing over time?
2. How does revenue compare with total cost?
3. Which departments contribute most to revenue?
4. Which sub-categories generate the highest gross profit?
5. How does performance vary by quarter?
6. Which sub-categories have the highest return quantities?

## Key KPIs

- Total Revenue: 281.75M
- Total Cost: 236.28M
- Net Revenue: 224.71M
- Gross Profit: 45.47M
- Gross Margin: 16.14%
- Return Rate: 33.20%

## Key Findings

- Department 1 contributes approximately 87.7% of total revenue.
- Injections generate approximately 21.13M in gross profit among the displayed sub-categories.
- Monthly revenue reaches approximately 28M at its peak during the displayed period.
- Injections have the highest displayed return quantity at approximately 0.16M.
- Overall gross profit is approximately 45.47M.
